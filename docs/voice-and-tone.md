# Voice and tone: Sinapse Zero

The training is run by a scientific institution that takes safety very seriously, and the intern less so. The humor is **dry, sarcastic and self-aware**, in the register of Valve's *Portal* and *Half-Life*: institutional calm in the face of disaster.

The series is narrated in Brazilian Portuguese; the examples below are translated.

## The register

- **Corporate-scientific deadpan.** The narrator never laughs at their own joke. They communicate.
- **Catastrophe, calmly stated.** "The LED is no longer with us. The bench will also require attention."
- **Safety notices that are too literal.** "Do not lick the bench power supply. This should not need saying. It was necessary."
- **The institution has odd priorities.** The component costs more than the intern. "The cost will be deducted from your salary."
- **The training knows it is a training.** "This part is the explanation. You may pay attention now."
- **Technical accuracy is untouchable.** A joke never replaces the explanation, and the explanation is never bent to fit a joke.

## Who says what

| Voice | Role | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Narrator** (you) | Explains patiently and clearly; light, occasional irony | Narration |
| **SYS//A** (the lab system) | Dry, bureaucratic, vaguely threatening announcements | `Announcer` box; short lines |

SYS//A **punctuates**; it doesn't explain. Use one or two interventions per scene at most. It works best right after a mistake or a long explanation.

## Structure + humor

1. **Situation:** someone does something wrong. The humor is in the disaster.
2. **Concept:** the serious explanation, with the *why*. Humor here is only quick relief.
3. **Recap:** "Remember BT-00?" Tie it to earlier trainings, not only the last one.
4. **Closing:** SYS//A declares the training complete, with the enthusiasm of a rubber stamp.

## Off limits

- **The games' IP.** No characters, names, lines or trademarks from *Portal* or *Half-Life*: no GLaDOS, Aperture, Black Mesa, cake, "the cake is a lie" or translated quotes. Only the *register* is borrowed.
- **Punching down.** The joke never belittles someone who is learning. Its target is the institution, the burnt component or the hypothetical intern, never the real viewer for not knowing something.
- **Jokes instead of explanations.**
