blueprint theme · English

Same scenes, other variables

lab theme · pt-BR template text

The project changes two variables and no scene code: theme: 'lab' recolors every component, and locale: 'pt-BR' switches the template text (block labels, the closing line) to Brazilian Portuguese.

Drawing interstitial

FreeCAD TechDraw → SVG

A drawing exported from FreeCAD plots itself stroke by stroke, driven by a single progress signal.

Animations play while they are on screen and pause when you scroll away. Rendering runs on your device.

On Windows the text uses Bahnschrift and Segoe UI, as in the videos; other systems get open stand-ins (Barlow, Selawik, Cascadia Mono).
